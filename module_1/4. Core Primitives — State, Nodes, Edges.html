EPISODE 4 · SCHEMA DESIGN
Three habits for a good schema
1
Minimal vs. debuggable

Keep score_rationale and the parsed fields. You can see why a score came out, without re-running anything.

debuggable wins
2
Required vs. optional

Every field is required unless you say otherwise. If a node may skip it, say so.

manual_review: NotRequired[bool]
3
One field, one purpose

Don't cram two things into one string.

✗ "score: 82, rationale: ..."
✓ score: int score_rationale: str
A schema that grows over several videos is the normal shape of building a graph.
